**Solmar Bay** is an original open-world crime game built for make.fun's
gta6-01 "Make GTA 6" competition. Danny Cole is out and owes fixer Mara Voss
one last job: steal cars, outrun the bay police, and earn your way out — one
job at a time.

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server (for example `node serve.ts`) and open
http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No network needed — every file the
game loads is inside `game/`.

## The game

- A procedural sun-soaked city: street grid, varied buildings, sidewalks,
  parks, lamps, a bay with a pier — day lighting with shadows.
- On foot: WASD + mouse look, sprint, building collision.
- Drivable cars: walk up, press E to steal, arcade driving, E to exit.
  Sedans, sports cars, vans, taxis — and police cruisers.
- Traffic AI on the street grid and pedestrians who flee from danger.
- Wanted system: stars rise with crimes, police cruisers pursue, bust and
  wasted states, wanted decays when you lay low.
- Combat: hitscan shooting with tracers and muzzle flash; armed gunmen shoot
  back.
- Five story missions with objectives, timers, rewards and a completion state.
- HUD: health, cash, wanted stars, objective, timer, speed, and a live
  minimap. Title, pause, game-over screens and a localStorage save system.
- Sound: fully synthesized WebAudio — engine hum, sirens, gunshots, cash and
  mission stingers. No audio files.

All names, characters, places and story are original. Nothing from
Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto series or any other commercial game.
